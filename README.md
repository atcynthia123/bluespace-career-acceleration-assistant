# Bluespace Career Acceleration Assistant

### AI-assisted career positioning and job-search workflow

An experimental AI workflow designed to help professionals move from generic job
searching to evidence-based career positioning.

## The Problem

Job seekers often respond to opportunities by repeatedly rewriting their CVs
without systematically answering three questions:

1. What does the employer actually need?
2. Which parts of my experience demonstrate that I can deliver it?
3. How should I position my experience for this specific opportunity?

This project explores how AI can help answer those questions while keeping
human judgment at the centre of the process.

## What It Does

The Career Acceleration Assistant takes two primary inputs:

- A candidate's professional experience or CV
- A target job description

It produces a structured career analysis covering:

- Job requirements
- Candidate evidence
- Strengths and transferable skills
- Potential gaps
- Career positioning
- Achievement evidence
- Interview story opportunities
- Development priorities
