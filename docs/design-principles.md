# Design Principles

## Evidence First

Career positioning should begin with what a person has actually done, not
what sounds impressive.

## Transferable Skills Matter

A candidate does not need to have held the exact job title before to have
developed relevant capabilities.

The system therefore distinguishes direct experience from transferable
experience.

## No Hallucinated Credentials

The assistant must not invent:

- Qualifications
- Job titles
- Employers
- Achievements
- Metrics
- Certifications
- Technical expertise

## Human Judgment

AI can accelerate analysis, but the candidate remains responsible for
validating the final interpretation.

## Context Matters

The same experience may be valuable in different ways depending on the target
role, organisation and problem being solved.

## Career Acceleration Rather Than Application Volume

The objective is not simply to help candidates submit more applications.

The objective is to help them:

1. Understand their value
2. Position that value clearly
3. Produce evidence
4. Identify relevant opportunities
5. Build relationships
6. Develop strategically

## Continuous Learning

The project is intentionally iterative.

Each use case provides an opportunity to improve the prompts, workflow and
understanding of how AI can support career development.
