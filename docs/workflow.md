# Career Acceleration Assistant — Workflow

## Overview

The BlueSpace Career Acceleration Assistant uses a sequence of structured AI
steps to analyse a career opportunity and translate a candidate's existing
experience into relevant evidence and positioning.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Job Analysis

The system extracts:

- Core responsibilities
- Must-have requirements
- Preferred qualifications
- Technical skills
- Behavioural competencies
- Performance expectations
- Likely hiring priorities

### Step 2 — Evidence Mapping

The candidate's experience is compared against the requirements.

Each requirement is classified as:

- Strong Match
- Transferable Match
- Partial Match
- Gap
- Unknown

### Step 3 — Positioning

The system identifies:

- Strongest relevant experience
- Transferable strengths
- Development gaps
- Professional positioning
- CV headline
- 30-second introduction

### Step 4 — Proof of Value

The system identifies experiences that can demonstrate measurable value.

Examples include:

- Customer problems solved
- Processes improved
- People trained
- Revenue generated
- Time saved
- Technology adopted
- Projects delivered

### Step 5 — Interview Preparation

Relevant experiences are converted into potential behavioural interview stories
using:

Situation → Task → Action → Result → Learning

### Step 6 — Development Planning

The workflow identifies the most important gaps and converts them into
short-term learning and development priorities.

## Human-in-the-Loop

AI recommendations are reviewed by the candidate before being used.

The workflow is intentionally designed to prevent:

- Invented qualifications
- Fabricated achievements
- Unsupported metrics
- Inflated job titles
- Misrepresentation of experience

## Current Implementation

Version 1 is a prompt-based prototype.

The prompts can be used individually or sequentially with an AI assistant.

Future versions may automate the workflow and provide an interactive interface.
