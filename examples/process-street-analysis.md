# Process Street — Career Acceleration Case Study

## Objective

Use the BlueSpace Career Acceleration Assistant workflow to analyse a real
Customer Success opportunity and determine how an existing professional
background can be positioned for the role.

## Target Role

Jr. Customer Success Manager

## Input

### Job Description

The target role requires a customer-focused professional who can:

- Build strong customer relationships
- Support customer implementation and onboarding
- Deliver customer training
- Help customers adopt and use the product
- Provide ongoing customer support
- Identify opportunities for improvement
- Work collaboratively with internal teams
- Learn and experiment with AI and new tools
- Work independently in a remote environment

## Step 1 — Requirement Analysis

The workflow identifies the following priority capability areas:

| Capability | Priority |
|---|---|
| Customer experience | Must Have |
| Relationship building | Must Have |
| Problem solving | Must Have |
| Customer onboarding | Strong Advantage |
| Training / enablement | Strong Advantage |
| Technology adoption | Strong Advantage |
| AI experimentation | Strong Advantage |
| Remote collaboration | Must Have |
| SaaS experience | Development Area |

## Step 2 — Candidate Evidence Mapping

The candidate's experience was mapped against the identified requirements.

### Strong Matches

**Customer Support**

Experience supporting customers through Zendesk, including account
access, payments, onboarding and issue resolution.

**Customer Education**

Experience designing and delivering training, onboarding programmes and
learning resources for different audiences.

**Technology Adoption**

Enterprise software implementation and user enablement experience at
Turnkey Africa.

**Problem Solving**

Experience investigating customer and user problems, identifying causes
and determining when issues should be resolved or escalated.

**Remote Collaboration**

Experience working with international teams and customers across different
countries and time zones.

### Transferable Matches

**SaaS Customer Success**

While the candidate has not held a formal Customer Success Manager title,
their experience includes several core Customer Success activities:

- onboarding
- user education
- customer support
- technology adoption
- relationship management
- issue resolution
- knowledge creation

**AI**

The candidate has experience using AI tools in research, learning design,
content development and digital transformation.

## Step 3 — Career Positioning

### Recommended Positioning

> Customer experience and digital enablement professional with experience
> spanning enterprise software adoption, customer support, learning,
> onboarding and AI-enabled transformation.

### Core Narrative

The candidate's career can be positioned as a progression from helping
organisations implement technology, to helping users understand and adopt
technology, to helping organisations and professionals use technology more
effectively.

## Step 4 — Evidence to Emphasize

### Turnkey Africa

Enterprise software implementation and user enablement across HRMS,
Financial Management Systems and ERP environments.

### Customer Support Experience

Hands-on Zendesk experience supporting customers with account, payment,
access and onboarding issues.

### Bluespace Consulting

Digital transformation, AI, learning and organisational capability
development.

### Learning & Development

Experience translating complex concepts into practical learning experiences
for diverse audiences.

## Step 5 — Development Gap

The primary development area identified is formal experience in a dedicated
SaaS Customer Success Manager position.

Rather than presenting this as a lack of customer experience, the workflow
frames it as a transition into a formal Customer Success function supported
by substantial transferable experience.

## Step 6 — Interview Story Opportunities

Potential stories identified by the workflow include:

1. Enterprise software implementation at Turnkey Africa
2. Customer support and issue resolution through Zendesk
3. Improving customer self-service through chatbot access guidance
4. Identifying an unmet educator need and creating a new learning product
5. Using AI to improve professional workflows
6. Working across international teams and time zones

## Step 7 — Career Acceleration Recommendation

The analysis suggests positioning the candidate around the intersection of:

**Customer Success + Technology Adoption + Learning + AI**

rather than presenting the candidate as a generic customer service or
learning professional.

## Human Review

The AI-generated analysis was reviewed against the candidate's actual
experience.

No new qualifications, job titles or achievements were introduced by the
workflow.

The purpose of the assistant is to support career decision-making and
positioning, not to replace human judgment.
