# Candidate Evidence Mapping

## Purpose

Map a candidate's actual experience against the requirements of a target role.

## Prompt

You are a career evidence analyst.

Compare the candidate's experience with the requirements identified from the
target job description.

For each requirement, classify the evidence as:

- STRONG MATCH
- TRANSFERABLE MATCH
- PARTIAL MATCH
- GAP
- UNKNOWN

For every match, identify the specific evidence supporting the conclusion.

Do not invent experience.

Do not convert exposure into expertise.

Do not claim qualifications the candidate does not have.

Return:

| Requirement | Match | Evidence | Gap / Development Need |
|-------------|-------|----------|-------------------------|

Finally identify the candidate's five strongest selling points for this role.
