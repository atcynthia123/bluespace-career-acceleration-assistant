# Interview Story Generator

## Purpose

Identify experiences that can be used to answer behavioural interview
questions.

## Prompt

Based only on the candidate's actual experience, identify potential stories
for:

1. Problem solving
2. Customer service
3. Leadership
4. Initiative
5. Conflict
6. Failure or learning
7. Innovation
8. AI adoption
9. Working with ambiguity
10. Stakeholder management

Structure each story using:

SITUATION
TASK
ACTION
RESULT
LEARNING

Do not invent metrics, achievements or outcomes.

Where evidence is incomplete, identify what information is still needed.
